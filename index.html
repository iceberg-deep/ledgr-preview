Why Ledgr

Most finance apps make money by seeing your data. Ledgr is built so it can't — the engine runs on your device, and the rules are yours to read and edit.

Private by construction

Your ledger lives in on-device storage and never touches our servers. It's not a promise we won't look — it's an architecture where we can't.

Transparent, not a black box

Categorization runs on a real double-entry ledger with rules you can see and edit — not an opaque model deciding where your money went.

Teaches you, with your numbers

Ledgr is designed to build real financial literacy — from budgeting basics upward — using your own actual cash flow, not generic quizzes.

The whole point

“You pay us, and we still can't see your data.”

We make money from the software, not from you. That's the deliberate inversion of apps whose revenue depends on seeing your data, selling it, and taking a cut of your savings.

The honest, precise claim: raw transactions never leave your device. The optional AI advisor sends only derived aggregates (totals, trends) — under your own API key, with explicit opt-in, and never through our servers. We don't claim “nothing ever leaves”; we claim your raw ledger doesn't.
